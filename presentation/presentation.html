The Problem

What students face

  • Course & career info is scattered across catalogs, advisors, websites.
  • Hard to know which courses actually build the skills a career needs.
  • Prerequisite chains & term scheduling are confusing to untangle by hand.

The gap

No single place that turns interests + completed courses into a concrete, ordered study plan and career roadmap.

Source: requirements.md · personas.md

Meet "Nam"

Who

  • Early 20s, mid-degree, picking next term's courses
  • Comfortable with web apps — laptop & phone interchangeably

Goals

  • Choose a career to aim for
  • See current skills vs. what the career requires

The Solution

A responsive, accessible (WCAG 2.1 AA) React app — three pages.

Login

Enter a username to create or load a profile. No password — prototype-grade session.

Landing (Results)

The core experience: select courses & interests, pick careers, view radar + study pathway.

Profile

View/edit saved courses, interests, careers, and named study plans.

Core Features

🔎 Catalog selection

Typeahead multi-select for completed courses, skills/interests, and careers.

📊 Required vs. Current radar

Overlays a career's required skills on Nam's current competency — auto-derived from completed courses. Toggle between careers.

🪜 Step-by-step pathway

Ordered recommendations that respect:

  • course prerequisites
  • skill-gap priority (close biggest gaps first)
  • conflict-free term scheduling
