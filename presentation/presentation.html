The Problem

What learners face

  • Course & career info is scattered across catalogs, advisors, and sites.
  • Hard to see which courses actually build the skills a career needs.
  • Prerequisite chains & term scheduling are painful to untangle by hand.

The gap

No single place that turns interests + completed courses into a concrete, ordered study plan and a career roadmap you can actually follow.

Meet “Oat”, the Learner

Who

  • One generic learner — covers university students, prospective students, and self-learners.
  • Comfortable with web apps; not necessarily a CS expert.
  • Mid-journey, deciding what to study next.

Goals

  • Decide confidently what to study next.
  • See a tangible link between coursework and careers.

The Solution — Three Pages

A responsive, accessible (WCAG 2.1 AA target) React app over a FastAPI backend.

Login /login

Enter a username to create or load a profile. No password — prototype session.

Landing / Results /

The core experience: pick courses & interests, choose careers, view radar + study pathway.

Profile /profile

View/edit saved courses, interests, careers, and named study plans.

Protected routes redirect to /login when there’s no active profile.

Core Features

🔎 Catalog selection

Debounced typeahead multi-select for completed courses (university + online), skills/interests, and careers.

📊 Required-vs-current radar

Overlays a career’s required skills on Oat’s current competency — auto-derived from completed courses. Toggle between careers; text/table alternative for accessibility.

🪜 Step-by-step pathway

Ordered course recommendations that respect:

  • course prerequisites
  • skill-gap priority — close the biggest gaps first
  • conflict-free term scheduling

When no clash-free plan exists, it surfaces why (unmet prereq or time conflict).

Real, Openly-Licensed Data

Catalog content

  • Courses from MIT OpenCourseWare, Harvard CS50, Stanford SEE + open online platforms.
  • Skills modeled on ACM/IEEE CS2023 knowledge areas.
  • Careers modeled on O*NET occupation data.

Done responsibly

  • Only openly-licensed sources; proprietary catalog text excluded.
  • Attribution preserved (CC BY / CC BY-NC-SA · O*NET CC BY 4.0).
  • No personal data; external content paraphrased for licensing compliance.
